The desk’s original “one issue, one fix” conclusion does not match its own computed views. This review uses the supplied support export dated 4 October 2026, not a live ticket-system query. The review date records when we read the export; it does not make the exported reports current.

The export has 427 rows but 412 unique report IDs: 15 overlapping rows must not be counted again. All 412 unique reports are filed under #4471. The supplied tenant-based logic assigns them to 2 causes; 38 intake tags disagree with those assignments.

Two problems need separate engineering tickets

Parser assumes month first

379 reports, across 11 tenants; received 2026-04-01 through 2026-10-04 in this export. The shared parser reads month first even when the tenant writes day first. Correct the parsing contract to respect the tenant's date convention and test ambiguous dates.

Booking-v3 gives a month-first hint to day-first tenants

33 reports, across 3 tenants; received 2026-04-17 through 2026-09-22 in this export. The form prints MM/DD/YYYY, but interprets the entry using the tenant's day-first convention. Correct the hint and input interpretation together; a shared-parser fix alone does not resolve this path.

5 reports still need investigation

The supplied classifier files the following 5 reports under parser. They remain included in that total. Their bodies describe a one-day change, an invite arriving before booking, or other timing anomalies. Tenant and channel routing do not prove the mechanism behind those symptoms. Ask for the original input, stored booking timestamp, output and timezone; split a further cause only when evidence supports it. Do not label these a confirmed timezone bug.
