The desk handles several products. Subject-only merging under accepted decision 0001 put separate date mechanisms in #4471 and hid a problem without its own ticket. Intake tags also disagree with tenant configuration. The reassessment contains computed evidence and uncertainty.

Decision replacing decision 0001

Use subjects to find candidate reports, then read the body and verify the mechanism using the product, tenant configuration, channel and observed behavior. Merge only when evidence supports the same cause. Different subjects can be duplicates; identical subjects need not be. Unknown tenants or unsupported symptoms go into investigation, with a request for missing evidence. The date-specific classifier is evidence for this incident, not a universal classifier for every product on the desk.

Rollout and consequences

The current triage page links to this record. Stop automatic merges on subject alone and review new reports manually.
