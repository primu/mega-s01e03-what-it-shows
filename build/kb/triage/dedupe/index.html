Triage: verify the cause before merging

owner support desk accepted reviewed 2026-10-28 · 0 days ago

The support desk handles all products, not only sched-prod. Keep one ticket per supported cause, preserving each unique report ID and its received/merged times. Subject matching finds candidates; it does not establish duplicates. This replaces the stale subject-only guidance under decision 0002.

Current rule

  1. Read the report’s body, affected product, behavior, input/output and relevant configuration.
  2. Compare evidence of the mechanism with candidate tickets. For date reports, resolve the tenant locale, booking form and channel; intake browser locale and tags are only guesses.
  3. Merge only when the evidence supports the same cause, even if the subjects differ.
  4. Keep different causes apart even when their subjects match. If the tenant, mechanism or evidence is missing, put the report in investigation and request the missing details.
  5. Count unique IDs once. Preserve contradictory evidence, recalculate after new exports, and assign an owner. A “known” label does not prove a fix.
